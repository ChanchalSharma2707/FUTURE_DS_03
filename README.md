# 📊 Task 3 – Bank Marketing Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is part of my **Data Science & Analytics Internship**. The objective of this task is to analyze **bank marketing campaign data** and understand customer behavior, campaign performance, and the factors that influence whether a customer subscribes to a term deposit.

The analysis was performed using **Microsoft Power BI** to create an interactive dashboard with meaningful visualizations and key business insights.

---

## 📂 Dataset

**Dataset:** `bank-full.csv`

The dataset contains information about customers and their responses to a bank's marketing campaign.

### Important Features

* **Age** – Age of the customer
* **Job** – Type of occupation
* **Marital** – Marital status
* **Education** – Education level
* **Balance** – Average yearly balance
* **Housing** – Housing loan status
* **Loan** – Personal loan status
* **Contact** – Communication method
* **Month** – Month of last contact
* **Campaign** – Number of contacts performed during the campaign
* **Previous** – Number of contacts performed before the current campaign
* **Poutcome** – Outcome of the previous marketing campaign
* **Y** – Whether the customer subscribed to a term deposit

## 📊 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of the bank's marketing campaign.

### Key KPIs

* 👥 Total Customers
* 💰 Customers Subscribed
* 📈 Subscription Rate
* 🎂 Average Customer Age

### Visualizations

The dashboard analyzes:

* Subscription by Job
* Subscription by Education
* Subscription by Age Group
* Subscription by Contact Method
* Campaign Performance
* Previous Campaign Outcomes
* Customer Demographics

### Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes filters for:

* Job
* Education
* Marital Status
* Contact Method
* Month
* Subscription Status

---

## 🔍 Key Findings & Insights

* Customer characteristics such as **age, job, education, and marital status** provide useful insights into subscription behavior.

* The **communication method** used by the bank plays an important role in customer responses.

* Customers with a **successful previous campaign outcome** show different subscription behavior compared with customers who were previously unsuccessful or not contacted.

* The number of contacts made during a campaign can be analyzed to understand **campaign effectiveness**.

* The dashboard helps identify customer segments that show stronger interest in **term-deposit subscriptions**.

* Interactive filtering makes it easier to compare customer groups and identify patterns in campaign performance.
